## Additional Prior Art Located

### Exact complete-bipartite cycle counts

S. Durocher, D. S. Gunderson, P. C. Li, and M. Skala,
*Cycle-maximal triangle-free graphs*, Discrete Mathematics 338 (2015),
includes the complete-bipartite cycle count

\[
\sum_{r=2}^{a}\frac{a!\,b!}{2r(a-r)!(b-r)!}.
\]

This directly confirms that the cycle formula used in this repository is
standard. It does not anticipate recognizing the Stern-Zamir construction
as a subdivision.

### Divisible and complete-bipartite subdivisions

N. Alon and M. Krivelevich, *Divisible subdivisions*, Journal of Graph
Theory 98 (2021), and O. Janzer, *The Extremal Number of the Subdivisions
of the Complete Bipartite Graph*, SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 34
(2020), study subdivision and divisibility phenomena in different
structural roles. They are relevant background, not direct antecedents for
the Section 8.1 normal form.
